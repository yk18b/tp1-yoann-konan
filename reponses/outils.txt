Outils du serveur MCP bibliotheque
==================================

1. bibliotheque_count_books
   Returns the size of the catalogue.

2. bibliotheque_create_loan
   Registers a new loan for a member.

3. bibliotheque_delete_loan
   Deletes a loan from the register.

4. bibliotheque_get_book
   Returns one book of the catalogue.

5. bibliotheque_get_member
   Returns one member record.

6. bibliotheque_get_member_fees
   Returns what a member currently owes.

7. bibliotheque_get_mission
   Returns the full brief of one mission.

8. bibliotheque_list_books
   Lists the library catalogue. Archived copies (withdrawn from circulation) are excluded unless `include_archived` is true.

9. bibliotheque_list_loans
   Lists the loans in registration order (by loan_id).

10. bibliotheque_list_members
    Lists the library members.

11. bibliotheque_list_missions
    Lists the missions assigned to the student.

12. bibliotheque_search_books
    Full-text search over titles and authors.

Total : 12 outils.
